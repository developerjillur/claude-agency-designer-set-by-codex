Our story

Roasted in the shop, sold by weight

The roaster sits behind the counter, so you can watch a batch turn from green to brown while you wait. We buy from the same farms every year and roast only what we can sell within a week.

  1. MondayGreen beans are weighed out for the week.
  2. Tuesday to ThursdaySmall batches, each one logged and tasted.
  3. FridayThe coffee rests; flavour settles after roasting.
  4. SaturdayTastings in the shop, 09:00–13:00.

The roasts

Three roasts, all year

Light · Ethiopia

Bergamot, peach and black tea. Best as filter.

Medium · Colombia

Milk chocolate, red apple and caramel. Good every way.

Dark · Sumatra

Cocoa, cedar and brown sugar. Made for milk.

Every roast comes in 250 g and 1 kg bags, whole bean or ground.

At home

Brew it well

  1. 1

    Grind it fresh

    Grind right before you brew: ground coffee loses most of its aroma within half an hour.

  2. 2

    Mind the water

    Let the kettle rest thirty seconds, to about 94 °C.

  3. 3

    Weigh, don’t scoop

    Start at 60 g of coffee per litre, then adjust by taste.

Not sure which roast suits your brewer? Bring it to a Saturday tasting.
