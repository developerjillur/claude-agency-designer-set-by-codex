Great coffee is roasted, not bought.

Ana Ruiz has roasted beans in a shop, a garage and a popcorn machine. This book shows you how to roast at home in twenty minutes, taste what changed, and do it better the next week.

Inside: three roast profiles to start with, a tasting log, and the one habit that stops coffee tasting flat.

Ana Ruiz is the head roaster at Tidewater Coffee Roasters.
