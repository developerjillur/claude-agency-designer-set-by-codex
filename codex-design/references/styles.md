# Style directions (starting points, not templates)

A direction fixes the decisions that make a set look designed: colour strategy, type pool, one signature motif,
texture, image treatment and what is forbidden. Pick one per brand or campaign (the client's brand wins where it
exists), write it into the job's direction contract, and keep it for the whole series. Rotate fonts within the pool
across a client's work so pieces do not converge. Never mix two directions in one piece.

## How to pick

- Start from the client's world (product, audience, market, voice), not from the category cliché (leaves for organic,
  shields for security, sparkles for premium, circuit globes for tech).
- Choose a colour strategy (craft.md §5): Restrained, Committed, Full palette or Drenched.
- Write one sentence of physical scene ("printed on uncoated paper in a sunny studio", "a night-time transit poster")
  to decide light vs dark, texture and contrast.
- Swap-the-logo test: if a competitor's logo fits the direction equally well, add a specific idea (their material,
  their place, their product's shape, their language).

## The directions

**1. Swiss Signal:** clarity, authority.
Best for: corporate, finance, consulting, infographics, LinkedIn, event programmes.
Colour: Restrained; neutrals + one signal colour (red, cobalt or a brand colour) ≤ 10 %.
Type pool: Hanken Grotesk, Schibsted Grotesk, Archivo, Public Sans, IBM Plex Sans (+ Plex Mono for data).
Layout: a visible 12-column grid, flush-left rag, big numbers, strong horizontal rules, generous white space,
asymmetric blocks. Motif: a single thick rule or a coloured block that anchors the headline. Texture: none.
Images: cropped tight, black-and-white or natural colour, never decorative. Don't: centre anything, add shadows.

**2. Editorial Chapters:** considered, story-led.
Best for: case studies, carousels, brochures, reports, B2B thought leadership, NGOs.
Colour: Full palette of muted fields (one per slide or section) + ink.
Type pool: Newsreader or Source Serif 4 display + Libre Franklin or Instrument Sans; Fraunces only with a reason.
Layout: magazine grid, oversized pull quotes, a statement slide in a carousel, captions and folios as functional
labels, image and text interlocking. Motif: chapter numbers only when there is a real sequence. Texture: optional
paper grain at 3–5 %. Don't: a tracked-caps eyebrow over every heading.

**3. Bold Committed:** energy, launch, offer.
Best for: product launches, ads, sale posts, events, fitness, FMCG.
Colour: Committed or Drenched: one saturated brand colour on 30–60 % + ink + one contrast accent.
Type pool: Archivo (expanded/condensed), Big Shoulders, Unbounded (short words), Bricolage Grotesque, Barlow Condensed.
Layout: huge type (headline 140–220 px on 1080), a product cut-out overlapping the type for depth, one offer badge
built from type (not a starburst). Motif: one geometric device taken from the product or logo. Texture: none or
fine grain. Don't: gradients as decoration, more than one badge, all-caps paragraphs.

**4. Print Texture:** tactile, human, crafted.
Best for: event posters, cafés and food, music, culture, festivals, occasion posts, youth brands.
Colour: two or three "inks" on paper (riso pink + blue, black + one spot colour).
Type pool: Big Shoulders, Archivo, Fraunces (SOFT), Young Serif, Anek Bangla for Bengali display.
Layout: halftone or two-ink photos, slight misregistration, cut-paper shapes, type as image. Motif: halftone dots or
a torn edge. Texture: paper grain 5–8 %, halftone, xerox noise (a deliberate look, never to hide provenance).
Don't: glossy 3D, glass, neon glow.

**5. Minimal Zine:** quiet confidence.
Best for: premium, architecture, galleries, fashion, quote cards, invitations.
Colour: 70–90 % bare paper tone; one accent covering 0.8–2.5 % of the canvas.
Type pool: Instrument Serif / Instrument Sans, Gloock, Libre Caslon, Jost, Albert Sans.
Layout: one anchor element, much air, small confident type, an off-centre placement. Motif: the accent as a single
dot, line or word. Texture: none or very fine grain. Don't: fill the space, add icons.

**6. Warm Paper Card:** friendly advice.
Best for: tip cards, quote cards, educational posts, wellness, local services.
Colour: paper tones with one deep ink colour; avoid the cream + italic serif + terracotta attractor.
Type pool: Lora, Source Serif 4, Nunito Sans, Figtree, Hind / Noto Sans Bengali for Bengali body.
Layout: a card with generous margins, a short headline, 2–4 lines of body, a small signature line. Motif: a
hand-drawn underline or corner mark (SVG). Texture: paper grain 3–5 %. Don't: emoji, clip-art icons.

**7. Data Card:** evidence.
Best for: stat cards, infographics, reports, LinkedIn documents.
Colour: one highlight colour + greys; categorical palettes only to encode data, colour-vision safe.
Type pool: IBM Plex Sans + Plex Mono, Public Sans, Source Sans 3, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next.
Layout: the insight as the headline ("Wait times fell 38 % in six months"), the chart as proof, direct labels (no
legend if possible), a source line. Motif: the highlighted bar or point. Texture: none. Don't: 3D charts, pie
charts with more than 4 slices, numbers without a source, identical stat tiles in a row.

**8. Photo-led Minimal:** the photo is the message.
Best for: hospitality, real estate, travel, lifestyle, clinics (with real photos), web heroes, covers.
Colour: taken from the photo; the brand colour as a small accent.
Type pool: one sans (Figtree, Hanken Grotesk, Albert Sans) or one serif (Newsreader); small confident sizes.
Layout: full-bleed photo, the calm zone holds the type, an eased scrim only where the contrast check asks for it,
logo small. Motif: none; the crop is the idea. Texture: the photo's own grain. Don't: text over faces, busy crops.

**9. Technical Framed:** precision.
Best for: B2B, engineering, developer tools, product specs, SaaS (without the SaaS clichés).
Colour: dark or light neutral + one electric accent (not the purple-blue gradient).
Type pool: Mona Sans, Host Grotesk, Funnel Display/Sans, JetBrains Mono for labels.
Layout: thin frames and rails that overshoot at the corners, measured annotations that label real things, blueprint
grid used sparingly. Motif: corner ticks. Texture: none. Don't: fake UI, glowing orbs, circuit globes.

**10. Pastel Geometry:** friendly learning.
Best for: education, children's services, health literacy, community programmes.
Colour: 3–4 soft tints with one deep ink for all text (tints never carry small text).
Type pool: Fredoka, Nunito, Baloo 2 / Baloo Da 2 (Bengali), Lexend.
Layout: simple shapes as containers, step numbers for real sequences, large friendly type. Motif: one shape family
(circles or rounded squares). Texture: none. Don't: rainbow palettes, clip-art, mixed icon styles.

## Thumbnail and cover headline overlays

For YouTube thumbnails and bold covers, the headline is an HTML overlay (never generated), cap height 12–18 % of the
frame, 2–4 words, line-height 0.9, tracking −0.01 to −0.02 em, stroke under the fill with `paint-order: stroke fill`.

| Overlay | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Beast | Anton or Archivo Black, all caps, white fill, black stroke 10–12 % of cap height, hard dark offset shadow |
| Fire | the same face, a yellow → orange → red fill (`#FFE24B → #FF9A1F → #FF2E2E`), dark stroke, a soft orange glow |
| Neon Lime | acid lime `#D4FF3F` fill, dark stroke ~12 px at 1280, a faint lime glow; only on dark plates |
| Clean Glass | a heavy sans (800) on a frosted pill: rgba(20,20,25,.45), 16 px backdrop blur, radius .2em; for calm, premium channels |
| Marker | black condensed caps on lime highlighter bars (`box-decoration-break: clone`) |

Use one overlay per channel as its signature; vary the photo, the expression and the words, not the overlay.

## Trends: what lasts (2025–2026 reports)

- **Durable:** human and imperfect signals (scans, xerox, grain, hand drawing, naive marks) as a reaction to AI
  sameness; type as the hero image; editorial/zine storytelling layouts; local cultural specificity (Bengali and
  Devanagari lettering, local craft motifs) done with locals, not as pastiche; clean "opt-out" minimalism.
- **Cyclical:** maximalism and collage; retro, lo-fi, 90s TV and Y2K; Neo-Deco and opulence.
- **Fads or risky:** gummy/jelly 3D, glass everywhere, surreal AI absurdity (raises the AI-look risk).
- **Trend colours** (Pantone 2026 Cloud Dancer, 2025 Mocha Mousse; Pinterest's cherry red, butter yellow, cool blue)
  are seasonal accents only, never the base of a brand.
