## 1. Transcreation principles

1. **Start from a brief, not the source text** (Smartling). The brief covers the intent, audience, feeling, action, what is fixed (brand, claim, price, legal) and what may change.
2. **Route by stakes.** Translate specs and legal lines, localise body copy, and transcreate headlines, hooks and CTAs (Phrase). Write natively when the idea can't travel (Translated).
3. **Give options.** Offer 2–3 per key line, each with a back-translation and a one-line rationale (Smartling).
4. **Target the locale, not the language.** es-ES/MX/AR, pt-BR/PT, zh-CN/TW/HK, ms/id and Gulf/Egyptian/Maghrebi Arabic are separate targets (Ulatus).
5. **Register first.** Fix the address form, dialect, script and code-mixing before drafting, then keep them across posts, ads and replies (inter-contact).
6. **Keep the idea, swap the vehicle.** Idioms, puns, references and proof get local equivalents. Check names for local meanings (gtelocalize).
7. **Review in context.** A spreadsheet hides whether "Run" addresses a woman (gtelocalize), so review the rendered design.
8. **Localise formats:** currency, separators, dates, clock and digits. German and French run up to 30% longer, so re-compose rather than shrink.
9. **Machine-translation tells:** English word order and sentence length; calques; "you/your" in every line (Anda, você, あなた); wrong or mixed formality; textbook register on social; words from the wrong variant; English punctuation; lost wordplay. LLM translation is "overly literal and unnatural" (arXiv 2503.04369).
10. **AI tells survive translation:** a calqued "not X, it's Y" (*No es solo X, es Y*), triads, slogan fragments, colon rhythm, hype adjectives, English Title Case in es/fr/pt headlines. Flag them when repeated or conspicuous (writing_quality.md).
11. **Dashes.** LLM English overuses em dashes where people would use commas, colons or parentheses (Wikipedia). Native dashes (see the table) also read as AI when overused: Brazilians call the 16th-century travessão a ChatGPT sign (CNN Brasil). Rule: use the local form, once per piece at most, never for rhythm. Never put an English "—" where the locale wants " – " (de), "——" (zh) or "――" (ja).

## 4. Gaps in codex-design, in priority order

1. **Transcreation workflow** (`references/copy.md`): brief → native draft → 2–3 options with back-translations → native review on the render. `copy.json` gains `locale`, `register` and `reviewer`.
2. **Register table per locale:** address form, dialect, script, code-mixing and formats. None exists; start from §2.
3. **Humanising lint:** craft.md has only a buzzword row (§7) and an em-dash note (§2). Add negative parallelism, triads, slogan fragments, colon rhythm, pronoun overuse, Title Case, non-English phrase lists and a read-aloud test.
4. **Copy and locale rubric:** the judge scores visuals and text accuracy only. Add hook, clarity, proof, voice, CTA, native naturalness (1–5) and zero AI tells, plus MQM categories with minor/major/critical severity.
5. **Punctuation and script checks:**
   - per-language quotes, dashes and spacing (French spacing, „“ vs «», ¿¡, full-width CJK);
   - Turkish casing;
   - bidi isolation;
   - Urdu Nastaliq and Tamil fonts;
   - zh-HK vs zh-TW.
6. **Voice:** `brand.json` holds only `is`/`is_not`. Add axes, tone by post type, a lexicon, the address form per locale and do/don't pairs.
7. **Hooks and CTAs:** a hook library (truthful gap, specific number, contrarian, before/after) and CTA verbs by objective, platform and locale.
8. **Captions:** only scattered lines (first 125 characters, CTA in the caption). Add per-platform structure, truncation, emoji, hashtags and per-locale captions with character counts.
9. **Accessibility:** alt text per carousel slide; long descriptions for infographics; no Unicode "bold" letters in captions; subtitles on video.
10. **Commercial calendar:** occasions.md lacks 11.11, Black/White Friday, Ramadan and Diwali seasons, Golden Week, lead times, and greetings in these languages.
11. **A/B variants:** `pairwise` picks before launch. Add one hypothesis and one changed variable per variant, and naming. Platform results outrank judge votes.
12. **Pillars and series:** 3–5 pillars, a series bible, and a copy ledger so hooks and CTAs never repeat.
13. **Trends:** visual only. Add a dated job-time check on formats, audio and memes, covering fit, rights, expiry and no tragedy.
